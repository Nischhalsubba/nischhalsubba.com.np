# nrs-content-and-cases

Each case: project intro, user/business problem, role and collaboration, constraints, journey/process, key decisions, state coverage, authentic screenshots with captions, implementation handoff, honest outcomes, related reading and relevant CTA. Never invent dates, metrics, research results, role scope, client quotes, approvals, shipping status or source screenshots. Blog: record SERP intent, original firsthand examples, clear thesis, substantive direct answers, no copied source phrasing, related case and service links, authorship/date accuracy and topical differentiation. For external public websites label capture date, current vs historical features, and boundaries of contribution. No fake backlinks or mass-produced city pages.

## Deliverables
Write evidence-backed changes or an audit record, including paths, date, validation command/output, actual blockers and clear follow-ups.
