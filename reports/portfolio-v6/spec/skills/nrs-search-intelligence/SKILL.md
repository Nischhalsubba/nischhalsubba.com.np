# nrs-search-intelligence

Start with connected Search Console for query/page/country/device, 28/90-day baseline, branded/nonbranded. Treat small samples as unstable; rare queries anonymized. Compare Nepal and US separately. Inspect actual Google/Bing SERPs, competing page formats and intent manually when browsing allowed. Paid Ahrefs metrics may be unavailable; label volume/KD/DR/CTR assumptions as unverified rather than invented. Map one primary intent per route. Monitor AI citations as separate observational evidence (not a universal AI ranking); Google's AI search needs standard SEO fundamentals, not special llms.txt schema.

## Deliverables
Write evidence-backed changes or an audit record, including paths, date, validation command/output, actual blockers and clear follow-ups.
