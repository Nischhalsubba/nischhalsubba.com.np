## Case-study narrative contract

Every flagship project should make these questions easy to answer:

1. What product or business problem existed?
2. Who was affected?
3. What constraints mattered?
4. What was Nischhal responsible for?
5. What decisions changed the experience?
6. What states, edge cases, or system behavior had to be designed?
7. What evidence can the reader see?
8. What shipped, changed, or was learned?

Do not invent metrics, research findings, user quotes, or impact claims.

## Content pillars

All public writing should reinforce one or more of these themes:

1. **Complex product UX** — roles, permissions, dense data, workflows, states.
2. **Design systems** — components, tokens, variants, responsive rules, governance.
3. **Design × engineering** — handoff, implementation constraints, production QA, design-to-code continuity.
4. **Trust-heavy product design** — fintech and Web3 actions, transactions, signing, verification, recovery, errors.
5. **Product reasoning** — tradeoffs, decision frameworks, before/after thinking, critiques, and lessons from real work.

## Content quality rule

Prefer one strong original insight over frequent generic posting. A useful post should contain at least one of:

- a concrete design decision,
- a tradeoff,
- a before/after comparison,
- a reusable framework,
- a failure or lesson,
- implementation detail that changes the design decision.
