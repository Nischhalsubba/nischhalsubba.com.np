# Sourcement — Resource Management Landing Page

## Overview

Sourcement is a resource management platform for Tarrif, as described in my September 2021 portfolio. I completed the landing-page design-to-development challenge within three days. This retrospective case study documents the recovered original interface and separates its visible design decisions from proposed improvements.

## Problem and contribution

The page needed to communicate a resource-management product quickly and provide a clear route to exploring it or starting a trial. My documented contribution covered design and development of the challenge. The original archive does not identify a commissioned commercial engagement, research participants, development stack or deployment outcome.

## Original interface evidence

The original 2538 × 1369 PNG was extracted unchanged from the embedded image on page 7 of Besides UI and UX.pdf (September 2021). Insert the verified original image here when it has a public asset URL; do not publish an image placeholder.

### Navigation and entry points

The navigation presents product, pricing, about and career links alongside contact and trial actions. This separates exploratory browsing from higher-intent conversion. A future iteration should test the labels, spelling and relative prominence of the two conversion routes.

### Hero and trial registration

The hero introduces the resource-management proposition, an email field and a green trial action. A desktop and mobile product illustration provides a glimpse of the scheduling interface. The original design includes a seven-day trial and a no-credit-card message; these are visible mockup copy, not verified present-day offer terms. The mockup also uses several third-party brand logos; these are historical design elements, not verified clients or endorsements.

### Feature presentation

A project-lifecycle section organizes sales pipeline, technologies and industries into tabs with three concise feature cards. The hierarchy shifts from product proposition to more detailed capabilities. In the recovered image, two feature cards repeat the same heading, and the trial action has a spelling error; these are opportunities for editorial refinement, not claims that fixes were shipped.

## Proposed validation and handoff

For a future iteration, ask prospective operations or resource-planning users to explain what the product does, locate pricing, identify the next step and understand the trial terms. Test navigation, form errors, keyboard access, contrast and responsive behavior. Document loading, submission success, invalid email and network recovery. No completed user research, conversion improvement or production deployment has been independently verified.
