## Overview

An independent 2021 redesign exploration of Clubhouse, published on Dribbble. The project is a personal concept, not commissioned work for Clubhouse.

## Design intention

The original rationale explores a more colorful visual language reflecting the social audio product's variety of clubs and rooms. I also designed a concept logo in Adobe Illustrator to complement the interface direction.

## Deliverable

The published visual exploration demonstrates mobile interface styling and a proposed brand identity. It does not establish that the concept was implemented or that user engagement improved.

## Problem framing and audience

The design exploration addresses the visual identity of an audio-first social experience. The original concept emphasizes the diversity and entertainment value of rooms and clubs. A useful follow-up question is whether a richer visual language can communicate that variety without making live conversations harder to discover.

The intended audience for evaluation would include people browsing for a conversation, returning members of a club, and first-time listeners. These are proposed evaluation groups, not researched audience segments.

## My approach and solution

The documented approach is a visual redesign and concept identity, including an original logo created in Adobe Illustrator. Color is used as an expressive branding direction rather than evidence of improved engagement. The original mobile artwork above is the delivered visual artifact; the following journey and interaction decisions are recommendations for extending it.

## Proposed listener journey

1. Discover: identify a relevant conversation or club from available topics.
2. Evaluate: understand the room topic, participants and whether the session is live.
3. Join: enter as a listener, with clear microphone and participation expectations.
4. Participate: understand how to request to speak and recognize moderation feedback.
5. Leave or return: exit safely and locate relevant future conversations.

A production design should communicate room identity through labels and hierarchy as well as color, keep audio controls visible, and make joining and leaving unambiguous.

## Proposed research questions

- How do you choose an audio room? Investigates the information needed before joining.
- What makes you comfortable participating? Identifies expectations around privacy and moderation.
- How do you distinguish listening from speaking? Evaluates state clarity.
- How would you recover if the audio connection drops? Tests recovery expectations.

## Accessibility, edge cases and evaluation

Test contrast, larger text, screen-reader labels, mute status, microphone permission denial, connection failure, empty room listings, and host moderation states. A production prototype should distinguish room status through both text and visual indicators. Evaluate task success, comprehension and confidence rather than assuming a more colorful interface increases retention.

## Outcome and reflection

The delivered work is an independent 2021 visual exploration and concept logo. No documented user sample, implementation, or measured engagement outcome is available. The next iteration would validate the discovery and participation flows before extending the visual system.
