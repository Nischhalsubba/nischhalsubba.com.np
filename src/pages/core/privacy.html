Privacy

What this portfolio collects and why.

This site is built to show design work and make it possible to contact me. It does not sell personal data or create advertising profiles.

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, the information you enter is sent through FormSubmit and delivered to my email. This can include your name, email address, selected enquiry type, message, source page and submission time.

Please do not send passwords, financial information, medical records or other sensitive personal data through the form.

How messages are used

Messages are used only to respond to job opportunities, project enquiries, portfolio questions or professional collaboration. They may remain in my email history so I can continue the conversation and keep a reasonable record of professional correspondence.

Analytics

The site may use privacy-conscious analytics or basic interaction events to understand which pages, projects and contact actions are useful. Analytics are not used to sell data or build third-party advertising audiences.

External services

Links and optional embeds can open services such as Figma, LinkedIn, Behance, GitHub and Uxcel. Those services apply their own privacy policies when you visit them.

Your request

You can request correction or deletion of information you previously sent by emailing hinischalsubba@gmail.com. I may need enough context to locate the relevant message.

Updates
