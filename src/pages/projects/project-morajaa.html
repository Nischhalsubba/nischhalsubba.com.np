PRODUCT CONTEXT

What needed to be made clearer

Morajaa needed a consulting website that could communicate services, sectors, and lead pathways with enough clarity for high-consideration B2B visitors.

My role

I designed page structure, content hierarchy, visual presentation, and a final Figma prototype for the website experience.

Focus 1

Premium service-site structure

Structured content and sections so visitors could understand the offer, compare options, and move toward contact.

Focus 2

Sector/service page hierarchy

Structured content and sections so visitors could understand the offer, compare options, and move toward contact.

Focus 3

Guided lead paths

Shaped the product experience around clearer hierarchy, practical UI decisions, and fewer ambiguous next steps.

Focus 4

Figma prototype

Turned the idea into a reviewable flow so decisions could be tested, shared, and discussed with less ambiguity.

OUTCOME

What the design made easier

  • Clearer entry points for the main user roles and use cases.
  • More explicit labels, hierarchy, and state language around important actions.
  • Reusable UI decisions that can scale beyond one screen.
  • A stronger hiring walkthrough because scope, artifacts, and constraints are visible.
