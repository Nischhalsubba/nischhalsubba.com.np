Useful brief

Tell me what needs to become clearer.

Good messages usually include the product, audience, timeline, current problem, and what kind of design help is needed. This saves everyone from the ancient ritual of 12 clarification emails.

  • 01

    Role or project type: full-time, contract, audit, redesign, or freelance design support.

  • 02

    Product context: what it does, who uses it, and where users currently struggle.

  • 03

    Design need: UX structure, UI polish, design system, prototype, handoff, QA, or website flow.

  • 04

    Links if available: website, app, Figma, Notion brief, product deck, or staging build.
