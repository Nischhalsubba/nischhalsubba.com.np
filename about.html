How I work

Useful design is not decoration. It reduces ambiguity for users and teams.

My early work at Gurzu, Diagonal Softwares, ThemeGrill, ESR Tech, and Tegzy gave me range across mobile apps, dashboards, websites, and content-heavy interfaces. Over time, that became a systems practice: reusable patterns, clean hierarchy, and practical UI decisions that survive development.

More recent work with Web3 and enterprise products sharpened my focus on trust-sensitive flows: onboarding, wallet context, transaction review, governance interfaces, verification states, and clear product explanation. I care about craft, but I care even more about whether the product becomes easier to understand, ship, and maintain.
