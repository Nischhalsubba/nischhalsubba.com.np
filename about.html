About

Product designer with a practical eye for systems, edge cases, and implementation.

I have worked across agency projects, product teams, WordPress products, Web3, fintech, SaaS, and service websites. The common thread is clarity: flows that make sense, interfaces that feel polished, and files engineers can actually build from.

How I work

I start by understanding the product, users, business context, and constraints. Then I map flows, states, hierarchy, and content before pushing visual polish. I care about the small things that make products easier to ship: empty states, error states, responsive behavior, naming, and handoff notes.

My resume shows a path from client-facing UX work into product systems: Gurzu and Diagonal Softwares for early UX foundations, ThemeGrill and ESR Tech for reusable UI and dashboards, Tegzy for design-system scale, Mokshya for Web3 product flows, and Idealaya for enterprise web software.

That mix helps me move comfortably between strategy and production. I can design a new app flow, clean up an existing dashboard, build a website direction, document a small design system, or collaborate closely with developers during implementation.
