THE PRODUCT PROBLEM

What needed to be made clearer

Yarsha needed mobile chat, wallet actions, blinks, groups, settings, payments, and AI-bot interactions to feel understandable without making every action feel risky or technical.

My contribution

I worked on product UX, high-fidelity mobile UI, prototype structure, and interaction states that help users understand what they are doing before they sign or send anything.

Focus 1

Messaging-first Web3 UX

Shaped the product experience around clearer hierarchy, practical UI decisions, and fewer ambiguous next steps.

Focus 2

Wallet and transfer review states

Made risk, status, and next actions easier to understand before users committed to an action.

Focus 3

Clear signing context

Made risk, status, and next actions easier to understand before users committed to an action.

Focus 4

Mobile design system patterns

Kept patterns reusable and documented enough for implementation instead of treating each screen as a one-off visual.
