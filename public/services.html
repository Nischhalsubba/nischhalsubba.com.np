Working process

A small number of deliberate stages.

  1. 01

    Context and constraints

    Define users, the business goal, current friction, technical limits, stakeholders and evidence already available.

  2. 02

    Flows and structure

    Map the journey, content priority, navigation, states and decision points before expensive visual work begins.

  3. 03

    Interface and prototype

    Create reusable patterns and realistic screens, then test important paths and edge cases through reviewable prototypes.

  4. 04

    Handoff and QA

    Document behavior, responsive rules, states and implementation notes, then review the built product against the intended design.

Practical questions

Before starting.

What should I send first?

A short product description, the current problem, useful links or screenshots, desired timeline and who will review the work.

Can you work with an existing design system?

Yes. I can use, extend, document or audit an existing system rather than replacing it for sport.

Do you collaborate with developers?

Yes. The work can include implementation context, responsive behavior, states, assets and design QA.

Do you take full-time product design roles?

Yes. The contact page can be used for full-time roles, contract work and focused product engagements.
