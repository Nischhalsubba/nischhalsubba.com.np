Best fit

Where I am most useful.

Bring me in when the product is understandable to the team but confusing to users, stakeholders, or engineers. Which, charmingly, is most products at some point.

  • Before build: Clarify flows, states, responsive behavior, content hierarchy and component decisions before engineering starts.
  • During redesign: Audit what is broken, separate symptoms from causes, and prioritize fixes that improve understanding and trust.
  • Before launch: Review UI, copy, accessibility basics, edge cases, empty states, error states and handoff quality.
  • For portfolio-style proof: Turn product work into case studies that explain scope, constraints, decisions and visible outcomes honestly.
