About Nischhal Raj Subba

I design digital products and websites where clarity matters: Web3 apps that need wallet and transaction confidence, SaaS dashboards that need workflow structure, fintech experiences that need trust, and service websites that need clearer content hierarchy.

My public work includes Yarsha, Mokshya.io, Hamro Idea, Morajaa, piHub, Masteriyo LMS, Zapp Today, Orkest HQ, Splashnode, Neverwinter Live Parser, Grid Labs Hosting, Zakra Furniture, Designerex, and sassBoilerplate. I describe work truthfully: whether I designed, contributed, built, structured, prototyped, audited, or explored the project.
